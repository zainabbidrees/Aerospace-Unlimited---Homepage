Part of ASAP Semiconductor

What the group gives you that a broker cannot.

Being part of a larger distribution group is only worth mentioning if it changes the answer you get. Here is where it does.

Reach into 2 billion part numbers

Our catalog is backed by the group's supplier network across aviation, defence, electronics and industrial hardware. When a part is not on our shelf, the search does not stop at our own inventory.

We hold stock ourselves

Inventory is physically on our shelves in Anaheim, inspected by our own receiving team. Items in stock and ordered before 3pm Pacific ship the same day, rather than being drop-shipped by a third party we have never inspected.

One quality system, audited

Purchasing, receiving inspection, traceability and counterfeit-avoidance run to a single documented system, subject to external surveillance audits — not to whatever a given salesperson decided that week.

Who we supply

The people already buying from us.

Airlines and operators

Line and base maintenance, rotable pool top-ups, and AOG cover out of hours. Documentation is supplied to the standard your receiving inspection applies, with 8130-3 or dual release where the item and register require it.

MROs and repair stations

Consumables and hardware for work packages, plus routing of removed units through our repair network. Quotes are itemised per line so they can be passed straight into a customer estimate.

Defence and government contractors

NSN, NIIN, FSC and CAGE-referenced procurement, with FAR and DFARS flow-downs accepted onto the order and export classification stated on the quote.

OEMs, integrators and FBOs

Shortage cover on production lines, obsolescence and end-of-life sourcing, and ground equipment for hangar and ramp operations.
